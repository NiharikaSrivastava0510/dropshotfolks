About us

Not obsessed, just present.

This one goes to those who hit snooze, thought about staying in bed, yet walked out the door regardless. To everyone discovering the biggest hurdle sits right at the start. Chasing shuttles becomes a reason, slowly uncovering shared grins during rallies, quiet support after rough matches, benches filling up at the local spot week by week. Not obsessed, just present. Time slips fast. Move. Connect. Return.

The Foundation of dropshotfolks

01.

Mental Health & Well Being

More than just physical movement; it's about mental clarity. We create a space to de-stress, recharge, and find your focus.

02.

Fitness & Physical Health

Keeping the heart rate up and the body sharp. We're dedicated to staying active and building strength together.

03.

Socials & Connections

It's the people here. We believe in building genuine relationships and a supportive network on and off the court.

04.

Competition & Win

Embracing the thrill of the game. We push each other to improve, compete fairly, and celebrate every victory.
